# Boot Resoling and Recycling Program

Worn-out boots don't have to end up in a landfill. Send them to us and we will resole them or recycle the materials.

## How to send boots in

Pack the boots in any sturdy box, include a note with your name and order number, and mail them to the CarvedRock Recycling Center. Resoling takes 3 to 4 weeks and costs $65 per pair.

## Recycling

Boots that cannot be resoled are recycled into trail surfacing and gasket material. Prepaid mailing labels for recycling are free; ask customer support for one.

## What we cannot accept

We cannot accept boots that are contaminated with chemicals or fuel, or boots with mold that has spread through the lining.
