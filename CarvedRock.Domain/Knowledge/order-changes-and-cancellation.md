# Order Changes and Cancellation

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself as long as it has not shipped. Open the user menu in the top right of the site, choose My Orders, and select Cancel order next to the order. You can also ask the CarvedRock assistant to cancel an order for you; it will ask you to confirm before anything is cancelled. You receive a cancellation email when it is done.

## Orders that have shipped

An order that has shipped cannot be cancelled. When it arrives you can return it under our Returns and Exchanges policy instead.

## Changing an order

Items in an order cannot be edited after it is placed. To change what you ordered, cancel the order while it is still unshipped and place a new one.

## Clearing your cart

Clearing your cart only empties the items you have not yet bought. It does not cancel an order you have already placed.

## Refunds for cancelled orders

Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.

## Need help?

If an order shows the wrong status, or you cannot cancel an order you believe has not shipped, contact customer support with your order number.
