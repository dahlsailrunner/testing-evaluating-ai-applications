# Tent Care and Setup

A tent is one of your most important pieces of shelter. A little care keeps it dry and strong.

## Choosing a site

Pitch on flat, well-drained ground, clear of sharp rocks and sticks. Avoid dry creek beds and low spots where water collects. Use a footprint, a groundsheet cut to the shape of the tent floor, to protect the floor from abrasion and moisture.

## Setting up

Stake out the corners first, then raise the poles. Pull the rainfly taut and stake out the guy lines so wind and rain run off instead of pooling. Face the narrow end of the tent into the wind.

## Reducing condensation

Leave vents open, even in cold weather, so moist air can escape. Do not cook inside the tent. If the fly is wet in the morning, wipe it down before packing.

## Seam sealing

New tents with untaped seams, and older tents that start to leak, need seam sealing. Apply seam sealer to the stitched seams of the fly, following the directions, and let it cure fully.

## Cleaning and storage

Never put a tent in a washing machine or dryer. Clean it with a sponge and mild soap. Dry it completely before storing, as a damp tent grows mildew within days. Store it loosely in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight, which breaks down the fabric over time.
