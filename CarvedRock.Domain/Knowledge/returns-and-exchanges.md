# Returns and Exchanges

CarvedRock Fitness wants you to love your gear. If something isn't right, you can return or exchange it under the policy below.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Boots and equipment must be unused and in their original packaging. Kayaks have a shorter window of 14 days from delivery and must be unused and undamaged.

## Trying on boots

Try boots on indoors, on a clean carpeted surface. Boots that have been worn outdoors, or show scuffs, mud or wear on the soles, cannot be returned.

## Final Sale items

Items marked Final Sale on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged. Clearance items are always marked Final Sale.

## Return shipping and refunds

Returns for defective or incorrect items ship free. For all other returns, a flat $7.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product, subject to availability. Start an exchange by contacting customer support with your order number.
