# Returns Policy (ARCHIVED 2023)

ARCHIVED - this policy has been superseded by the current Returns and Exchanges policy and is kept for reference only. Do not use it for current orders.

## Return window (old)

Under the 2023 policy, most items could be returned within 60 days of delivery, and kayaks within 30 days.

## Return shipping (old)

Return shipping was free on every return, whatever the reason. A restocking fee of 10 percent applied to opened equipment.

## Refunds (old)

Refunds were issued as store credit by default. Refunds to the original payment method had to be requested in writing.
