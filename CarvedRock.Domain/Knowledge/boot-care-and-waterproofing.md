# Boot Care and Waterproofing

With regular care, a good pair of boots lasts for many seasons.

## Cleaning

After a muddy hike, knock off loose dirt and scrub with a soft brush and lukewarm water. Remove the insoles and laces first. Do not use detergent, which can strip the waterproofing treatment.

## Drying

Let boots air dry at room temperature, away from heaters, fires and direct sunlight. Stuff them loosely with newspaper to absorb moisture and replace the paper when it is damp. Heat can crack leather and weaken the glue that holds the sole.

## Re-waterproofing

If water stops beading on the surface, it is time to re-waterproof. Do this every 3 to 6 months of regular use. Use a wax or cream treatment on leather boots, and a spray-on treatment on synthetic or fabric boots. Apply it to clean, damp boots and let them dry fully before use.

## Storage

Store boots in a cool, dry place with the laces loosened. Do not store them in a sealed plastic bag or a hot car trunk.

## Insoles and laces

Replace insoles about once a year, or sooner if they feel flat. Replace frayed laces before they snap on the trail.
