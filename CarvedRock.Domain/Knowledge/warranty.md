# Warranty

CarvedRock products are covered against manufacturing defects for the periods below, starting at the delivery date.

## Boots

Boots are covered for 1 year against manufacturing defects such as sole separation, failed stitching and leaking waterproof membranes. Normal wear, resoling, damage from misuse, and damage from storing boots wet or near direct heat are not covered.

## Kayaks

Kayak hulls are covered for 5 years against cracking, delamination and structural failure. Fittings such as hatches, footbraces, seats and deck lines are covered for 1 year. Damage from impacts, from transport without proper cradles, or from prolonged sun exposure is not covered.

## Equipment

Camping and paddling equipment, including tents, sleeping bags and accessories, is covered for 90 days against manufacturing defects.

## Making a claim

Contact customer support with your order number and clear photos of the problem. We will repair, replace or refund the item at our discretion. Warranty claims are separate from returns: they apply after the return window has closed.
