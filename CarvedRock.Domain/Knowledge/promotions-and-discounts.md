# Promotions and Discounts

## Promo codes

You can use one promo code per order, entered at checkout. Promo codes cannot be combined with each other or with another offer. Codes do not apply to shipping or freight fees.

## Seasonal sales

We run sales on seasonal gear, such as winter boots in spring and kayaks in autumn. Sale prices are shown on the product page and applied automatically.

## Clearance and Final Sale

Clearance items are marked Final Sale. Final Sale items cannot be returned or exchanged, so check the size and details carefully before you buy.

## Newsletter offers

Subscribers to the CarvedRock newsletter receive occasional offers by email. Offers have an expiry date and can only be used once per customer.

## Expired and invalid codes

An expired code cannot be reactivated. If a valid code is rejected at checkout, check that you have not already entered another offer, then contact customer support.
