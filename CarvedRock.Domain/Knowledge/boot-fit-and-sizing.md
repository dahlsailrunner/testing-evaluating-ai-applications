# Boot Fit and Sizing Guide

A well-fitting boot prevents blisters and sore feet. Use this guide before you order.

## Measuring your feet

Measure your feet in the late afternoon, when they are slightly larger. Stand on a sheet of paper, trace each foot, and measure heel to longest toe. Use the larger foot to choose your size.

## What a good fit feels like

You should be able to wiggle your toes freely, with about a thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe when standing. Your heel should stay snug without lifting more than a few millimeters when you walk. Nothing should pinch across the widest part of your foot.

## Socks

Try boots with the socks you will actually hike in. Midweight wool or synthetic hiking socks are the usual choice. Never fit boots over thin cotton socks.

## Half sizes and width

Most of our boots come in half sizes. If you are between sizes, go up a half size, especially for long descents. Wide widths are available on selected styles, shown on the product page.

## Breaking in

New boots need 10 to 15 hours of walking to mold to your feet. Start with short walks around the neighborhood before taking them on the trail.
