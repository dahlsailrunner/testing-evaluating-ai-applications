# Shipping and Delivery

## Processing time

Orders ship within 1 to 2 business days after they are placed. You receive an email with tracking information as soon as your order ships.

## Delivery speeds and cost

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and is free on orders over $75; below that it costs $6.95. Expedited shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and costs $14.95.

## Kayaks

Kayaks ship by freight carrier, not parcel post. Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and adds an $89 freight fee. The carrier calls to schedule a delivery appointment, and someone must be present to receive the kayak.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. We do not currently ship anywhere else, and we cannot ship to PO boxes.

## Tracking and delivery problems

Use the tracking link in your shipping email to follow your package. If a package arrives damaged or does not arrive within 10 business days of the estimated date, contact customer support with your order number.
